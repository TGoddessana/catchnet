# catchnet design principles

Every screen in catchnet is held to three principles. When a choice is unclear, pick the option that serves them best.

## 1. Usable without learning

Someone who has never seen catchnet should know what each screen is and what to do next. Plain words over jargon, familiar controls, nothing that needs a manual.

## 2. Never alarming

An error tracker is full of bad news. Say it calmly: quiet colors, clear sentences, and no alarm where none is needed.

## 3. The same rule everywhere

The same thing looks and behaves the same way on every screen. A person who learns it once can use it everywhere.
